Project overview:
=================
My current project is the Bigloop Data Platform at ZF. It is an end-to-end data platform that processes vehicle telematics, signal data and claim information from multiple automotive OEM's.

1. Data comes from multiple OEM's such as BMW, Audi, JLR and Stellantis.
2. The platform processes:
    Vehicle/component signal data
    Time-series/time-history data
    Warranty and claim information
3. The Objective is to derive:
    Component health
    Damage Analysis
    Remaining Useful Life
    Fleet-level behavior
    Warranty/Claim insights
    Reuses vs Replacement decisions
    The output is consumed by R&D and business stakeholders

The objective isn't simply to move data from Bronze to Gold. The objective this platform is to turn vehicle and claim data into information that can support Engineering decisions and circular-economy decision.
